# Questlyn — Product Brief

## Product Vision

Questlyn is a personal life management and self-development system
built around an RPG experience.

It connects real-life goals, tasks, habits, skills, activities,
wellbeing, and personal achievements into one coherent system.

The user's RPG character is an independent game character whose
development reflects the user's real-life progress.

Questlyn should help users understand their progress, develop
themselves intentionally, and make everyday actions contribute
to meaningful long-term development.

## Product Name

Questlyn

## Product Category

Life RPG / Personal Productivity / Self-Development

## Product Principle

Real life comes first. Gamification exists to make real progress
visible, understandable, and motivating — not to replace reality.
