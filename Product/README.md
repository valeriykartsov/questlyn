# Questlyn

Questlyn is an offline-first personal life management and self-development
application that turns real-life goals, tasks, habits, activities, and
achievements into an evolving RPG adventure.

The player develops a personal RPG character whose progression reflects
their real-life actions, skills, attributes, and experiences.

## Product identity

- Product name: Questlyn
- Product category: Life RPG / Personal Development
- Primary platform: iOS
- Initial architecture: Offline-first modular monolith
- Development approach: Design-first, iterative delivery
- Implementation assistant: Claude Code

## Naming status

Questlyn is the approved working product name.

Trademark availability, domain availability, and App Store naming
availability have not yet been legally verified. This verification
must happen before public release and commercial branding commitments.
